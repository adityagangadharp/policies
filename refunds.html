REFUNDS POLICY

1. Overview

We aim to ensure customer satisfaction. If you are not satisfied with your order, you may be eligible for a refund based on the conditions below.

2. Eligibility for Refund

A refund may be issued if: • Order was not delivered • Order was significantly delayed (beyond estimated delivery time) • Wrong or missing items received • Food quality issues reported within a reasonable time (30–60 minutes of delivery)

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will NOT be issued for: • Change of mind after order preparation has started • Incorrect order details provided by customer • Minor delays caused by external traffic or weather conditions • Fully consumed orders

4. Refund Processing Time

• Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days • Refunds are returned to the original payment method only

5. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued if: • Part of the order is missing • Replacement items were not possible • Service quality was partially affected

6. Refund Investigation

Each refund request is reviewed based on: • Order history • Delivery logs • Restaurant confirmation • Payment status

7. Contact

For refund issues, customers may be required to provide: • Order ID • Registered email or phone number • Description of issue
