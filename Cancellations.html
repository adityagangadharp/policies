CANCELLATION POLICY

1. Order Cancellation Window

Orders can be cancelled only before the restaurant starts preparation.

2. After Preparation Starts

• Cancellation is not guaranteed once preparation has started.
• Partial refund may be issued based on order progress and preparation stage.

3. Auto-Cancellation

Orders may be automatically cancelled in the following cases:

  • Payment failure
  • Restaurant rejection of the order
  • System or technical issues

4. Important Notes

Cancellation eligibility depends on real-time order status. Once an order enters preparation or dispatch stage, cancellation options become limited.
