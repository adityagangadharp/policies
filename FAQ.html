🤖 Frequently Asked Questions

1. Where is my order?

You can track your order in the app under “My Orders”.

2. How long does delivery take?

Typically 20–45 minutes depending on location and demand.

3. How do I request a refund?

Go to order details and click “Request Refund” or contact support with your order ID.

4. Can I change my order?

Once an order is confirmed, changes are not possible.

5. How do I contact support?

Use the in-app support or provide your order ID for assistance.
