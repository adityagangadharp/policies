DELIVERY POLICY

1. Overview

We aim to deliver all orders within the estimated delivery time shown at checkout.

2. Delivery Time Estimates

  • Standard delivery: 20–45 minutes
  • Peak hours: 45–75 minutes
  • External delays may apply during high demand or weather disruptions

3. Order Tracking

Customers can track their orders in real time once the order is dispatched.

Tracking includes:

  • Delivery partner name
  • Live status updates
  • ETA updates

4. Delivery Status Definitions

  • Preparing: Order is being prepared by restaurant
  • Out for Delivery: Order assigned to delivery partner
  • Delivered: Order successfully delivered

5. Delivery Delays

Delays may occur due to:

  • High order volume
  • Weather conditions
  • Traffic congestion
  • Restaurant preparation delays

6. Failed Delivery

If delivery fails due to incorrect address, customer unavailability, or contact failure, the order may be marked as failed or returned.

7. Contact Support

For delivery issues, please provide:

  • Order ID
  • Delivery address confirmation
  • Issue description
